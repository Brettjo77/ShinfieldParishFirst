# Shinfield Parish First: interim logo kit

Interim choice by Brett, 27 Nov 2026: **Concept B "Field & path"** in **Charcoal & Copper** (chosen 27 Nov 2026 evening, replacing Damson & Copper), until the steering group meets (about mid-December 2026). Treat as provisional.

Notes
- The lockup SVGs use live text in Bricolage Grotesque. Use the PNGs anywhere that font is not installed, or load it from Google Fonts on the web.
- Copper #B8642E is for large text and graphics. Use #9A4F1F for body-size text and buttons on light backgrounds (contrast).
- Green #A5BA5E is a hint only. Never a background or headline, so material is never mistaken for Shinfield Parish Council's.
- Every printed or online campaign item needs an imprint (who promoted it and on whose behalf).
